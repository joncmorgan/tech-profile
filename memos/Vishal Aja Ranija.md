**Memo to File Re: ERA Conference Session on Circular Economy**
**Core Summary**
This memo summarises a presentation by Vishal Aja Ranija (RMIT) at the ERA's Reshaping Existing Buildings conference. The talk compared the advanced state of circular economy principles in Finland's construction legislation against Australia's comparatively undeveloped approach.
**Key Points**
- **International Comparison:** The Finnish Construction Act was highlighted as a leading example of integrating circular economy principles, far surpassing current Australian standards.
- **Australian Position:** Australia's construction industry is considered stagnant in this area, with its understanding of the circular economy largely limited to basic recycling rather than more sophisticated circularity concepts.
- **Industry Contact:** The Sustainable Builders Alliance was mentioned as a potentially valuable industry group.
