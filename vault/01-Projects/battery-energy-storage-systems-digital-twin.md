# Battery Energy Storage Systems (BESS) Digital Twin

## Context
Developed for local government clients looking to right-size battery energy storage systems and evaluate financial viability under various commercial tariff structures.

## Execution
- Agentic Tooling & Container Architecture: Built as one of Jon's first personal experiments with AI agentic tooling, prototyped in VS Code. Wrapped the NREL System Advisory Model (SAM) engine into a custom Python codebase packaged into a container for on-demand ephemeral execution.
- Demand Profiling Module: Simulated diurnal and monthly variations across HVAC, lighting, and general electrical demand.
- Solar Generation Module: Factored in solar panel sizing, orientation, efficiency, degradation factors, and inverter losses.
- Battery Storage Module: Evaluated charging/discharging rates, round-trip efficiency, and total capacity.
- Tariff & Cost Analysis Module: Compared complex commercial tariff structures to evaluate financial payback.
- Automated Reporting: Streamlined automated compilation of technical outputs and client-ready reports.
