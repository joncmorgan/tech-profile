# City of Melbourne Sustainable Building Standards & Resilient Refuges Strategy

## Context
Partnered as lead technical sub-consultant to Nation Partners for the City of Melbourne to overhaul municipal building standards, embedding climate adaptation, critical event resilience, and sustainability targets across council-owned assets.

## Execution
- Resilient Infrastructure Guidelines: Formulated strategic protocols for retrofitting existing facilities and designing new builds to withstand future climate extremes, including bushfire smoke events and severe heatwaves. Addressed mechanical service adaptation, backup/off-grid power generation, and solar-battery integration.
- Community Heatwave Refuge Design: Defined architectural, thermal, and HVAC criteria to convert select municipal assets into public heatwave refuges, ensuring safe indoor air quality, passive/active cooling endurance, and power resilience for vulnerable populations during extreme weather emergencies.
- Standards Alignment: Recommended minimum sustainability performance targets using the Green Star and BESS frameworks, balancing long-term climate resilience with carbon mitigation objectives.
