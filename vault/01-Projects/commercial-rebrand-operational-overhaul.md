# Commercial Rebrand & Operational Overhaul

## Context
Navigated macroeconomic market headwinds by proactively restructuring professional services and client communications.

## Execution

- Technical Communication Standardisation: Standardised client-facing technical language across all service streams to improve clarity and engagement.
- Digital Content Strategy: Optimised digital messaging (LinkedIn and technical content strategy) to articulate practice capabilities and thought leadership.
