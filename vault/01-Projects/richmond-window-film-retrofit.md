# Richmond Window Film Retrofit

## Context
Building in Richmond, Melbourne, suffering from overheating problems, creating potential legal exposure (thermal discomfort claims from occupants). Client was a project manager seeking to retrofit external window film as the remediation.

## Execution
- Conducted technical analysis and performance modelling to test whether an external window film retrofit would address the overheating problem.
- Modelling demonstrated the proposed film solution would resolve the issue, and this analysis was used to secure planning approval for the retrofit.

## Outcome
- Planning approval granted.
- Multi-hundred-thousand-dollar window film contract secured and executed.

## Resilience gap (not yet closed)
No follow-up monitoring or verification was commissioned to confirm the retrofit worked in practice: actual post-retrofit thermal comfort, cooling loads, long-term film durability, or degradation over a 5-10 year horizon. Solution was delivered and approved, but there is no feedback loop tracking real-world effectiveness over time — a live example of the monitoring/verification gap in current practice.
