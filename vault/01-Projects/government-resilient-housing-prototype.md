# Government Resilient Housing Prototype

## Context
Partnered directly with an architectural practice to design and optimise a state government-backed resilient housing prototype constructed on the Mornington Peninsula.

## Execution
- Digital Twin & Envelope Modelling: Built the digital twin and computational models to inform building envelope performance parameters.
- Heatwave Climate Splicing: Mined historical Bureau of Meteorology (BOM) datasets against official heatwave definitions and spliced those sequences directly into EnergyPlus weather files (.epw) to rigorously simulate building performance under severe heatwave conditions.
- Physical Build: Evaluated building resilience under extreme stress testing to guide envelope parameters, resulting in a completed physical build.

Unverified: Jon believes this may be one of relatively few built examples of this kind of climate-adaptive housing prototype in Australia, and that it may have received some form of recognition or award — neither has been confirmed. Do not state as fact until checked.
