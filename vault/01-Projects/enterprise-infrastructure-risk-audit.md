# Enterprise Infrastructure Risk Audit, Systems Formalisation & Role Delegation

## Context
Engaged an external IT and systems auditing consultant to conduct a rigorous deep-dive review into the practice's sprawling digital infrastructure. The primary drivers were operational risk mitigation, mapping team data interactions, and addressing critical key-person risk: single-handedly managing an unmanageable full-stack individual contributor (IC) workload (spanning sysadmin, DevOps, UI/UX, software architecture, and database operations). The overarching objective was to systematically deconstruct and formalise the entire digital ecosystem to identify tasks suitable for operational delegation or offshoring, thereby reducing overheads and protecting senior engineering capacity.

## Execution
- Full-Stack Infrastructure Deconstruction: Audited and mapped the end-to-end digital ecosystem, spanning bare-metal work servers, cloud infrastructure, containerised workloads, relational and operational databases, and automated reporting toolchains.
- Standardised IT Notations & Formal Taxonomy: Systematically categorised, labelled, and documented all digital pipelines and system dependencies using formalised IT engineering notations to ensure full institutional transparency.
- Operational Risk & Data Workflow Auditing: Examined how internal engineering teams interacted with shared data stores, source files, and computational models to identify failure points, security vulnerabilities, single points of failure, and data integrity risks.
- Responsibility Uncoupling & Delegation Matrix: Disentangled high-value strategic architecture from operational maintenance. Mapped out which functions required dedicated senior IC oversight versus tasks that could be standardised, delegated, or offshored.
- Role Specification & Operational Job Descriptions: Authored targeted job descriptions, technical capability briefs, and standard operating procedures (SOPs) for newly defined modular IT, DevOps, and administration roles to facilitate targeted recruitment and offloading.
