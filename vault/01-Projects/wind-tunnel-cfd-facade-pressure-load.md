# Wind Tunnel & CFD Facade Pressure Load Integration

## Context
Evaluating structural wind pressure and cladding load dynamics on complex structures by integrating physical wind tunnel pressure tap data with high-resolution CFD numerical models.

## Execution
- Dynamic Sensor Mapping: Processed, normalised, and mapped dynamic wind tunnel pressure spectra onto high-density 3D structural meshes.
- Aerodynamic & Safety Assessment: Evaluated peak velocity pressure distributions, aerodynamic shedding, and localised suction loads for structural and facade safety compliance.
