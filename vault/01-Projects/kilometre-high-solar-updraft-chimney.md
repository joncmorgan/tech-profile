# Kilometre-High Solar Updraft Chimney

## Context
Architecting the fluid dynamics and thermal-updraft simulation pipeline for an extreme-scale 1 km solar power tower featuring a 3 km glass skirt.

## Execution
- Literature Synthesis & Physics Modelling: Extensively reviewed foundational technical literature to synthesise the governing physics and architect modular numerical routines.
- Ground Heat Transfer: Modelled heat transfer from air flowing under the glass skirt to the surface, coupled with a 1D heat transfer calculation down to stable ground temperature to simulate diurnal daytime heat storage and nighttime release.
- Canopy Solar Transmission: Calculated dynamic solar radiation transmission through the glass canopy as a function of sun angle.
- Aerodynamic Losses: Modelled friction and flow losses across the extensive skirt geometry, alongside momentum removal calculations as air passes through the power generation turbines at the base of the chimney.
- Atmospheric Thermodynamics & Ambient Effects: Factored in atmospheric lapse rates over the 1-kilometre height to account for pressure/temperature differentials between ground level and the chimney summit, alongside ambient wind effects at the top.
- Cross-Disciplinary Collaboration: Partnered closely with structural engineers evaluating physical constructability and facade engineers specialising in the complex skirt structural arrangement.
