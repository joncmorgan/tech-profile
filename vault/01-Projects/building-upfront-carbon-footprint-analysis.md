# Building Upfront Carbon Footprint Analysis

## Context
Whole-of-building embodied carbon quantification and regulatory rating submission delivered for major builder partner Hutchinson Builders ("Hutchies").

## Execution
- BOQ Ingestion & Standardisation: Ingested, cleaned, and standardised complex contractor Bills of Quantities (BOQs), verifying engineering units of measure across thousands of line items.
- Assembly Categorisation: Categorised elements into core building assemblies: substructure, superstructure, facade, and primary services.
- Data Sanitisation Rules: Engineered data-sanitisation rules to eliminate double-counting (e.g., separating structural rebar tonnage from reinforced concrete volume calculations).
- EPD Linking & Rating Submission: Linked cleaned material quantities to Environmental Product Declarations (EPDs) and material carbon emission factors within specialised SAS analysis platforms to deliver official rating submissions for Green Star accreditation.
