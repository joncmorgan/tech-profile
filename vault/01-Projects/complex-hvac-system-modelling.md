# Complex HVAC System Modelling & NABERS Commitment Agreements

## Context
Over 10 years of specialised HVAC system simulation, focused on formal NABERS Commitment Agreements that allow building owners to market pre-construction environmental ratings (e.g., NABERS 5-Star).

## Execution
- High-Fidelity EnergyPlus Simulation: Configured and executed predictive EnergyPlus thermal energy simulations engineered to replicate real-world operational building physics as closely as possible.
- Off-Axis & Degraded Risk Scenarios: Modelled degraded performance scenarios required for risk validation (e.g., fan schedule drift, chiller efficiency degradation, stuck control valves, and 24/7 running zones).
- Complex HVAC & Generation Schemes: Modelled complex mechanical plant including Variable Air Volume (VAV), Variable Refrigerant Flow (VRF), split systems, and inline PV solar generation with intricate metering layouts.
- Independent Regulatory Auditing: Successfully passed rigorous independent audits by accredited third-party NABERS assessors across multiple high-stakes commercial projects.
