# Low-Income Public Housing Asset & Energy Retrofit Platform

## Context
Auditing low-income public housing stock across Victoria to establish the empirical data foundation for state-funded heating, energy efficiency, and solar PV upgrade programs.

## Execution
- Mobile-First Ingestion Framework: Built a custom data collection framework on the Zoho low-code ecosystem, enabling field auditors to capture physical building enclosure details, simple residential heating equipment (e.g. basic electric element space heaters), and occupant survey responses via mobile devices.
- Qualitative Occupant Experience Capture: Integrated questionnaires into the audit workflow to capture tenant lived experiences, thermal comfort challenges, and specific household needs across vulnerable populations.
- Utility Billing & Data Integration: Ingested and cleansed historical utility billing data alongside physical asset conditions within a relational database schema.
- Privacy & De-Identification Safeguards: Engineered automated data-scrubbing routines to separate personally identifiable tenant information (PII) from physical asset records for regulatory compliance and downstream analytics.
- Tailored Upgrade Recommendations: Analysed combined asset conditions, lived-experience feedback, and billing data to recommend and prioritise high-yield capital retrofits (thermal shell improvements, split-system heat pumps, solar PV) across residential units.
- In-Home Monitoring: Installed temperature loggers in a subset of homes (~10) to establish a baseline and a post-intervention dataset for measuring actual improvement. Primary purpose was informing case studies on how occupants actually used their homes and the indoor environment they experienced, not just quantifying the retrofit's thermal performance.
