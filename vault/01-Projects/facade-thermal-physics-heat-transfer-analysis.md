# Facade Thermal Physics & Heat Transfer Analysis

## Context
Detailed thermal bridging and assembly performance analysis for high-performance architectural envelopes.

## Execution
- 2D Framing Heat Transfer (THERM): Conducted 2D steady-state heat transfer modelling of facade framing components (transoms, mullions) using LBNL THERM under NFRC reference conditions to calculate accurate linear thermal transmittance and frame U-values.
- Glazing Performance Analysis (WINDOW): Utilised LBNL WINDOW to model complex single, double, and triple-glazed configurations, evaluating solar heat gain coefficients (SHGC), centre-of-glass U-values, and the impact of low-emissivity (low-E) coatings.
