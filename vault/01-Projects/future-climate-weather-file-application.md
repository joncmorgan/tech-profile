# Future Climate Weather File Application & Stress Testing

## Context
Moving beyond static historical baselines to evaluate true long-term asset resilience under projected climate change scenarios.

## Execution
- Dynamic Energy Simulation: Applied synthetic future climate weather files developed by specialist teams to dynamic energy simulation models.
- HVAC Operational Stress Testing: Quantified forward-looking operational questions, determining how much additional energy HVAC systems would consume under projected extreme future conditions.
- Free-Running Overheating Quantification: Quantified the increased rate of overheating in free-running buildings if architectural designs remained unadapted.
