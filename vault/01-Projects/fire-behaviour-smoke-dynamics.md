# Fire Behaviour, Smoke Dynamics & Compute Infrastructure Architecture

## Context
Partnering with fire engineering teams to optimise computational fire modelling workflows and provision high-performance computing infrastructure.

## Execution
- Simulation Workflow & Tooling: Configured fire and smoke dynamics simulations using regulatory-approved software (NIST FDS). Built a custom Rhino 3D front-end interface to accelerate geometric setup within FDS’s strict rectilinear grid constraint.
- Interdisciplinary Egress Integration: Delivered fire and smoke arrival timeline outputs (visibility, temperature, toxic gas concentrations) to feed directly into external human movement and crowd evacuation simulations performed by separate egress specialists.
- HPC Cluster Architecture: Designed, specified, and deployed an 8-node headless Linux compute cluster managed via a batch queuing system (qsub, qhold), dramatically accelerating batch processing times for fire modelling and Australasia-wide building physics simulations.
- Enterprise Systems Administration: Maintained cluster hardware, managed networking throughput, and enforced security protocols in direct collaboration with enterprise IT.
- Technical Leadership: Held the formal title of Australasia Building Physics Lead with two direct reports; provided informal technical leadership and mentoring across ~10 further staff embedded in other teams (mechanical, facades) throughout APAC.
