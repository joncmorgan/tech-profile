# Stadium Pitch Microclimate & Cumulative PAR Modelling

## Context
Delivered pitch microclimate, solar access, and Photosynthetically Active Radiation (PAR) assessments across approximately half a dozen major stadia both while at Arup and subsequently as an independent consultant and subcontractor.

## Execution
- 3D Stadium Geometry & Overshadowing: Built detailed 3D digital models of stadium bowls, grandstands, and roof canopies to model precise diurnal and seasonal shading patterns cast across the playing surface.
- Turf Agronomic Criteria: Liaised directly with specialist turf consultants to establish species-specific PAR threshold targets, accounting for the distinct radiation requirements of warm-season (summer) and cool-season (winter) turf grasses.
- Cumulative Sky Formulation: Developed a cumulative sky methodology derived from local meteorological climate files, integrating direct beam and diffuse solar radiation components into a consolidated radiometric model.
- Monthly Pitch Radiation Mapping: Ran cumulative radiation simulations across the playing surface grid to calculate spatial and temporal PAR distribution on a monthly basis, identifying chronic shadow zones and quantifying PAR deficits.
- Supplemental Lighting Rig Optimisation: Formulated operational strategies to bridge the radiation deficit using mobile artificial growth lighting rigs. Calculated the minimum required rig count, spatial positioning, operational run times, and rotational sequencing schedules across pitch zones to minimise client capital and operational expenditure while maintaining turf viability.
