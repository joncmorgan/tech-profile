# Tunnel & Enclosed Car Park Ventilation (Rail & Automotive)

## Context
Airflow distribution, jet fan design, and contaminant concentration assessments for enclosed/underground multi-level car parks and preliminary assessment of diesel emissions in rail tunnels.

## Execution
- Car Park 3D Geometry & Contaminant Modelling: Built 3D computational geometries of underground and multi-level car parks. Determined vehicle contaminant source terms by establishing fleet average emission rates (specifically carbon monoxide, CO) from historical vehicle emissions data.
- Traffic Flow & Queue Integration: Collaborated with traffic engineers responsible for road network connections to determine incoming/outgoing traffic rates and projected queue lengths, using this data to define spatial distribution of contaminant emission sources within the model.
- Jet Fan Placement & Airflow Induction: Positioned and oriented mechanical impulse jet fans across the floor plates to drive bulk air movement, eliminate stagnant zones, and sweep exhaust gases towards dedicated mechanical extract shafts. Factored in boundary conditions such as natural ventilation openings and prevailing winds where present.
- Exposure Threshold Verification: Calculated steady-state carbon monoxide concentrations across pedestrian circulation zones to verify whether pollutant levels breached allowable regulatory exposure thresholds.
- Rail Corridor Assessment: Conducted preliminary evaluations examining diesel exhaust contaminant behaviour within rail tunnel environments.
