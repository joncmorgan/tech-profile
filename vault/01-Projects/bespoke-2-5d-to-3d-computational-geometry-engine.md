# Bespoke 2.5D to 3D Computational Geometry Engine

## Context
Architecting a custom procedural geometry engine to convert 2.5D building definitions into articulated 3D physical models for thermal and physical simulations.

## Execution
- 2.5D Architectural Definition to 3D Extrusion: Built routines to take 2.5D building plan definitions—including footprints, eaves, external shading devices, glazing bands, and roof profiles—and automatically convert them into watertight 3D geometries.
- Rapid Model Generation: Designed the pipeline to dramatically accelerate model creation, allowing designers and modellers to define building elements in plan and generate 3D simulation-ready geometry near-instantaneously.
- Calibrated Simulation Complexity: Selected an appropriate level of geometric complexity tailored to simulation requirements, balancing rapid generation against analytical fidelity while avoiding the computational overhead of full 3D CAD modeling environments.
