# Daylight, Solar Access & Visual Comfort Simulation

## Context
Delivered dozens of daylight availability, solar access, and visual comfort assessments across commercial office developments, aged care facilities, and multi-residential projects over many years. Work spanned two distinct objectives: regulatory compliance and human-centric design optimisation.

## Execution
- Compliance & Benchmark Metrics: Modelled baseline daylight metrics to meet statutory planning and Green Star requirements, evaluating Daylight Factor (DF), Daylight Autonomy (DA), and compliance against Illuminating Engineering Society (IES) standards.
- Design Optimisation & Visual Comfort: Conducted seasonal daylight and internal glare assessments to resolve functional design challenges: evaluating visual comfort in commercial offices to eliminate disabling glare on computer visual display units (VDUs); assessing direct daylight hours in aged care facilities to help ensure residents had sufficient natural light to comfortably read newspapers adjacent to windows; and guiding architectural massing, sizing, and geometric proportioning of internal light wells and light courts.
- Optical Physics & Envelope Materiality: Modelled solar trajectories coupled with seasonal sky brightness distribution algorithms to accurately represent local atmospheric conditions. Calibrated glass optical properties, deploying advanced angle-dependent transmission, reflection, and absorption models for high-performance facades alongside simplified visible light transmittance (VLT) algorithms for regulatory submissions, and assigned internal surface reflectances across floors, walls, and ceilings.
- Computational Strategy & Runtime Optimisation: Navigated commercial compute constraints within the Radiance synthetic imaging engine, where rigorous ray-tracing on large models can take multiple days. Formulated practical simplification and abstraction methodologies to run accurate, robust simulations within realistic commercial delivery windows (~10-minute compute targets) without sacrificing necessary physical fidelity.
