# Victorian Local Government Technical Capacity Building & BESS Framework

## Context
Multi-year capacity building initiatives delivered for the Municipal Association of Victoria (MAV) and the Council Alliance for a Sustainable Built Environment (CASBE) to empower local government statutory planners.

## Execution
- Non-Residential Engineering Review Training: Developed and delivered comprehensive technical training programs (conducted in 2025) for local government sustainability planners across Victoria. Trained planners on auditing complex non-residential energy modelling submissions, understanding thermal simulation workflows, verifying engineering assumptions, and identifying quality control red flags prior to permit approval.
- BESS Framework Integration: Collaborated closely with CASBE under the MAV umbrella to support and refine the Built Environment Sustainability Scorecard (BESS) framework, standardising environmental assessment tools and technical evaluation criteria across Victorian municipalities.
- BESS Testing & Evaluation: Provided direct input into testing and evaluating how the BESS tool itself performed in practice — feedback on the framework's own mechanics, not just applying it to projects.
