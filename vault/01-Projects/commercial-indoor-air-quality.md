# Commercial Indoor Air Quality, HVAC Distribution & Cleanroom Aerodynamics

## Context
Computational fluid dynamics (CFD) modelling to evaluate internal air distribution, contaminant clearance, and indoor air quality across commercial buildings and specialised cleanroom environments.

## Execution
- HVAC Distribution & Air Circulation: Modelled 3D internal airflow regimes across varying mechanical delivery systems—including underfloor or displacement ventilation systems and VRV/overhead diffuser arrangements—simulating supply, return, and exhaust configurations.
- Contaminant Dispersion & IAQ Benchmarking: Simulated internal contaminant dispersal and air circulation patterns, evaluating local age of air and tracking carbon dioxide concentration profiles against statutory 400-1000 ppm thresholds to avoid stagnant pockets.
- Cleanroom Aerodynamics: Conducted CFD simulations of cleanroom environments to assess laminar airflow integrity and particle clearance dynamics.
- Interdisciplinary HVAC Optimisation: Partnered directly with mechanical design teams to iteratively optimize diffuser locations, supply velocities, and exhaust return grille placements.
