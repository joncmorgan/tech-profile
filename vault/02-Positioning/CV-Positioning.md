## Target roles

## Summary
Building performance and sustainability engineer with close to 30 years' experience helping built-environment leaders navigate physical and climate risk — spanning applied building physics, environmental engineering, and full-stack software development. Co-directed an independent consulting practice while designing local-first computational tools that stress-test assets and decisions against a changing climate. Comfortable owning a problem end-to-end — from the physics, to the data pipeline, to the interface a client actually uses.

- Commercial & Operational Ownership — co-ran an independent ESD consulting practice for nearly a decade, closely involved in service-line profitability, cost management, strategy, branding and team leadership.
- Systems Architecture & Full-Stack Development — architects and builds custom software end-to-end, spanning UI, data pipelines, containerisation and local-first DevOps.
- Systems Engineering & Diagnostic Modelling — aerospace/defence-trained systems engineer; applies formal modelling method (abstraction, parameterisation, verification, validation) across aerospace, offshore, aviation, fire-safety and building-physics domains.
