# CV Positioning

> Reworked 24 Sep 2026 to fix the mismatch identified by market research: there
> is no Director-level "building physics" title in the Australian market.
> Senior titles cluster around Climate Resilience, Sustainability and
> Performance Engineering — building physics is real, deep expertise, but it
> belongs as supporting evidence, not the headline claim. This version leads
> with Systems Architect / Climate Resilience and demotes building physics to
> a domain-expertise line further down.

## Headline
Systems Architect — Climate Resilience & Building Performance

## Target roles
Director / Principal — Climate Resilience & Building Performance · Sustainability Leadership · Systems Architecture & Digital Twin Advisory · Independent or Associate Consultant

## Summary
Systems architect and engineer with close to 30 years' experience helping built-environment leaders navigate physical and climate risk — spanning applied building physics, environmental engineering, and full-stack software development. Co-directed an independent consulting practice while designing local-first computational tools that stress-test assets and decisions against a changing climate. Comfortable owning a problem end-to-end — from the physics, to the data pipeline, to the interface a client actually uses.

## Quote
"Climate change is outpacing the built environment's ability to adapt. Meeting that gap takes systems thinking and rigorous engineering — translating physical risk into decisions that protect the people and assets exposed to it."

## Core competencies
- Climate Resilience & Adaptation — deep technical grounding in heat and wind-driven building physics: heatwave stress-testing, thermal comfort modelling, and CFD/wind-tunnel analysis feeding into major architectural and structural design decisions.
- Regulatory & Rating-Framework Delivery — NCC Part J, Green Star, NABERS, BESS and JV3 compliance pathways delivered commercially at scale (Co-Perform); operates inside a framework to a pass/fail or star-rating outcome, not just a modelling exercise — the same discipline the ASRS/ISSB-driven roles now emerging in the market will need applied at asset level.
- Commercial & Operational Ownership — co-ran an independent ESD consulting practice for nearly a decade, closely involved in service-line profitability, cost management, strategy, branding and team leadership.
- Systems Architecture & Full-Stack Development — architects and builds custom software end-to-end, spanning UI, data pipelines, containerisation and local-first DevOps.
- Systems Engineering & Diagnostic Modelling — aerospace/defence-trained systems engineer; applies formal modelling method (abstraction, parameterisation, verification, validation) across aerospace, offshore, aviation, fire-safety and building-physics domains, not just buildings.
