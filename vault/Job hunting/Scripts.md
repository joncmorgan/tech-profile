# How to apply for a job

Write to us at careers@energyfuturesfoundation.org with:

1. Your CV, LinkedIn profile or another concise account of your work.
2. A note of no more than 600 words answering:

- Which part of EFF’s mission or which policy problem most motivates you, and why?
- Tell us about a difficult question you turned into analysis or a policy proposal that helped someone make a decision. What was your contribution, what did the evidence show and what changed?
