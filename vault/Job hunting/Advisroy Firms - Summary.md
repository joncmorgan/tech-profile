## On the "concise positioning statement" in the research
The research ends with its own suggested outreach language ("I lead
climate-resilient building-performance work at the point where hazard
analytics, building physics and owner investment decisions meet...").
**This is Perplexity's draft, not vetted language** — it hasn't been through
the same accuracy pass as `CV-Positioning.md` (e.g. "I lead ... work" reads
as a present-tense claim about work not yet underway). Don't use it verbatim
in outreach without running it past the same standard applied to everything
else in `02-Positioning/`.
