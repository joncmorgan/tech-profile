## 5. Portfolio Segmentation

### Segment A — Government / institutional ultimate clients (7 projects)
Real reporting pull-through; expect formal sustainability documentation as a contract deliverable.

- **CIT Woden** — ACT Government via Major Projects Canberra. Targeting **6 Star Green Star**, all-electric, with the façade explicitly named as a contributing element.
- **Australian War Memorial** (2 rows) — Corporate Commonwealth entity, captured by the **Commonwealth Climate Disclosure policy** rolling out FY2024-25 → FY2026-27.
- **Cowra Hospital** — NSW Health Infrastructure has briefed industry on Green Star certification **plus new embodied-carbon reporting requirements**, in partnership with GBCA. Targets net zero 2050 and −70% by 2035.
- **Cross River Rail** — Delivery Authority targets **IS "Excellent" (ISC v1.2)** Design and As Built at both program and package level; already issues an Environmentally Labelled Products Information Sheet, which functions as an EPD expectation.
- **Logan Hospital** — Metro South Health, under Queensland's Clean Economy Jobs Act 2024 targets.
- **Carseldine Village** — **5-Star Green Star Communities**, 100% net-zero-energy terraces, minimum 7-star NatHERS. SGCH reports under the Community Housing ESG Reporting Standard and applies modern-slavery contract clauses.

### Segment B — Listed / institutional owner subject to AASB S2 (1 project)
- **One One One Eagle Street** — the **only confirmed AASB S2 reporter** in the portfolio. Holds 5.5-star NABERS Energy (base building) and Climate Active carbon-neutral certification. Expect **assurance-grade Scope 3 data requests** on remedial and diagnostic work.

### Segment C — Private build-to-sell (9 projects)
Geocon/WOVA, The Markets Residences, ARC Residences, Fabbrica Fitzroy, both Deicorp projects, Vera Apartments, 235 Bourke Street.

- No mandatory corporate climate reporting confirmed for any of them.
- End owners are purchasers and owners corporations, not a reporting entity.
- **Geocon** is the only one with a substantive published policy: no new gas connections, recycled materials, heat-island reduction.
- Pressure here is **market- and planning-driven**, not corporate-disclosure-driven.

## 6. Key Takeaways

### The sleeper requirement: NSW Sustainable Buildings SEPP
This is the single most important finding for commercial positioning. It applies to **all three NSW rows regardless of ownership type**:

- Embodied emissions must be reported at **both DA and CC stage**.
- Residential pathway: **BASIX Materials Index** — glazing is explicitly in scope.
- Alternative: **NABERS Embodied Emissions Materials Form**, covering ≥80% of materials, A1–A3 only.
- **Implication:** this creates a façade-data obligation on private developers who have *no* corporate reporting duty at all — a segment that would otherwise be assumed low-pressure.

### Regulatory anchors verified
- **AASB S2 mandatory climate reporting** — Group 1 from 1 Jan 2025; Group 2 from 1 Jul 2026; Group 3 from 1 Jul 2027. Thresholds: >500/250/100 employees; ≥$1bn/$500m/$25m assets; ≥$500m/$200m/$50m revenue; asset owners ≥$5bn AUM.
- **Commonwealth Climate Disclosure** — Tranche 1 FY2024-25 through Tranche 3 FY2026-27.
- **ACT** — net zero by 30 June 2045 (Climate Change and Greenhouse Gas Reduction Act 2010).
- **Queensland** — −30% by 2030, −75% by 2035, zero by 2050 (Clean Economy Jobs Act 2024).
- **Modern Slavery Act 2018** — statement threshold $100m revenue.

### Data correction identified
"HMC" listed against **235 Bourke Street is the builder, not HMC Capital**. The actual asset owner is **Futuro Capital**. This materially changes the reporting-pressure assessment for that row and should be corrected in the source project register.

## 7. Actionable Insights

### Immediate commercial actions
1. **Correct the project register** — update 235 Bourke Street to reflect Futuro Capital as asset owner and HMC as builder.
2. **Prioritise EPD readiness for Cross River Rail** — the Environmentally Labelled Products Information Sheet is already in circulation; this is an active, not future, requirement.
3. **Build an NSW embodied-carbon service line** — BASIX Materials Index and NABERS Embodied Emissions Materials Form capability directly monetises the SEPP obligation across Cowra, Marquet & Mary, Falcon & Alexander and Vera.
4. **Prepare assurance-grade data for GPT/GWOF** — One One One Eagle Street remedial work will attract Scope 3 requests that must withstand external assurance.
5. **Position Green Star credit documentation as a deliverable** on CIT Woden (6 Star target), Carseldine Village (5-Star Communities) and Cowra Hospital.

### Façade-specific ESG evidence worth standardising
Anticipated data and document requests across the majority of these clients:

- Environmental Product Declarations (EPDs) for glass, aluminium, framing and sealants
- Embodied carbon per m² of façade area (A1–A3 as a minimum boundary)
- Recycled content percentages and material provenance
- Thermal performance evidence under **NCC 2022 Section J** and Whole-of-Home
- Condensation management documentation
- Glass and aluminium recyclability / end-of-life pathways
- Green Star credit documentation packs
- Modern slavery supply-chain declarations for clients above the $100m threshold

### Segmentation logic for future bids
- **Government-procured work** → assume Green Star / IS rating + embodied carbon reporting as baseline scope; price it in.
- **Listed or institutional owners** → assume AASB S2-driven, assurance-grade Scope 3 data requests.
- **Private build-to-sell** → low corporate duty, but check the **planning instrument** in the relevant jurisdiction; NSW is materially more demanding than ACT, QLD or VIC.
