**Memo to File Re:** Conversation with Craig Roussac, Buildings Alive
A discussion was held with Craig Russo of Buildings Alive regarding the increasing importance of verified building performance data, particularly in the context of upcoming ASRS reporting requirements for real estate investment trusts. The conversation also touched upon market competition, the role of major consulting firms, and the potential impact of AI on data analysis services.
### Key Points
*   **Data Verification for ASRS:**
    *   Mr Russo confirmed that Tier 1 real estate investment trusts will likely require a higher level of data verification for reasonable assurance in their ASRS reporting.
    *   He believes the extent of this verification will be driven by competitive pressure, as entities will aim to match or exceed their peers' standards.
*   **Role of Major Consulting Firms:**
    *   He anticipates that Group One entities will likely be required, directly or indirectly, to engage one of the "big four" consulting firms (EY, Deloitte, KPMG) for their ASRS compliance.
*   **Business Model Concerns:**
    *   Mr Russo expressed some concern that advancements in AI could potentially replace the human intelligence his team currently applies to building data streams, impacting his business model.
*   **Market & Industry Observations:**
    *   **Director Sign-off:** He found the question of whether reporting data would be of sufficient quality for director sign-off to be interesting but did not provide a definitive answer.
    *   **UK vs. Australia:** He opined that UK buildings are generally not managed as effectively as Australian buildings, attributing this to a lack of performance-based competition.
    *   **NABERS:** He highlighted the role of programmes like NABERS in driving competition and improving overall building performance outcomes in the Australian market.
