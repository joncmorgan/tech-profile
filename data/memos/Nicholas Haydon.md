**Memo to File Re: Nicholas Haydon (Schneider Electric) on Technology & People**
**Core Summary**
This memo summarises a presentation by Nicholas Hayden of Schneider Electric regarding the intersection of building technology and personnel. The central argument is that while data and analytics solutions exist, the primary challenge is ensuring the right people have timely access to this information to optimise building performance. This gap hinders energy reduction, capitalisation rates, and NABERS ratings.
**Key Points**
*   **Core Problem:** Data from building systems is not reaching the facility managers who need it to make informed decisions. A poll of facility managers revealed only 6% had access to both energy profile data and an HVAC analytics solution.
*   **Key Opportunities:** Effective use of real-time telemetry and control strategy optimisation can drive significant energy reduction. This directly improves a building's capitalisation rate and its NABERS rating.
*   **Schneider Electric Solutions:** The presentation mentioned specific products designed to address this data gap. These included "EcoCare," a service focused on data and performance engines, and "EcoStruxure."
