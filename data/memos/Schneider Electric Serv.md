Memo to File Re: Schneider Electric Services
A discussion was held with Nicholas Hayden of Schneider Electric regarding their data logging and advisory capabilities. Their primary model involves capturing building data through their proprietary controllers and loggers, which feeds into their dedicated platform.
### Key Points
- **Proprietary System:** Schneider Electric uses its own equipment (controllers, energy/data loggers) to capture data, which flows into its own platform.
- **Advisory Services:** They offer advisory services for building portfolios, even those with non-Schneider equipment. Their technical team excels at analysing building management system (BMS) data to identify performance issues and recommend upgrades.
- **Data Validation:** Schneider Electric does not guarantee the validation or verification of data ingested from third-party sources or equipment outside their own system.
