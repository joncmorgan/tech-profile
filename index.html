Compliance & Operations Top ↑

The evolution of mandatory sustainability disclosures, carbon reporting, and ESG frameworks establishes rigorous, auditable standards for corporate reporting. Aligning physical operations with compliance frameworks requires a specialised understanding of both building physics and data engineering—connecting physical assets, utility metering setups, thermal plant dynamics, and embodied carbon metrics directly to the balance sheet.

Mitigating transition risk requires moving past speculative checklists and implementing high-integrity physical modeling—simulating dynamic load curves, battery storage parameters, and local dynamic tariffs.

With a 30-year engineering background, I approach compliance, carbon accounting, and building optimisation as an integrated systems and data challenge. My focus is on bridging the gap between physical operational realities and corporate reporting—building automated, verifiable pipelines to ensure absolute data integrity and mitigate risk.
