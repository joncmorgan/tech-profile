Why This Background Matters for AASB S2 Corporate Compliance

The introduction of mandatory AASB S2 compliance establishes strict financial auditing requirements for sustainability reporting. Sustainability teams frequently struggle with manual spreadsheets that introduce error risks, while IT departments often lack familiarity with physical building management systems (BMS), utility meters, or plant operations.

With a 30-year engineering background, I approach compliance as a data and systems problem. I focus on bridging the gap between physical building performance and corporate reporting—building automated, verifiable pipelines to ensure rigorous data integrity.
